New Section Wireframe

Build with FairLend
Bring us the property. We will help build the equation.

From early intent to construction financing and takeout strategy, FairLend helps shape the land, scope, capital, draw plan, professional path, permit and MLI readiness, and exit around one financeable project.

You do not need the whole team, a perfect plan, or prior build experience before you talk to us. If you already build, keep your focus on the site while we help carry the financing and business equation around it. If you are exploring a garden suite, laneway suite, multiplex, or infill project for the first time, we can help make the path legible before expensive decisions harden.

Primary CTA

Map my build financing

Bring a property, plan, or early idea. We will help identify what has to be true before the numbers can work.

01 / Property + feasibility

Site, land basis, zoning path, housing form, unit mix, and buildable area.

02 / Capital + draw plan

Construction budget, borrower capital, private financing, draw schedule, and working capital.

03 / Team + readiness

Contractor, consultant, professional, supplier, permit, and CMHC MLI Select readiness path where applicable.

04 / Exit + takeout

Sale, refinance, rental stabilization, insured takeout preparation, and long-term financing direction.

Included operating advantage

This planning layer is built into the way FairLend approaches construction financing. It is a reason to finance with us, not another consultant you have to stitch onto the project later.

Transition Into Next Section

FairLend's thesis is simple: a profitable build is not only a construction problem, it is a business equation. Builders already know how to build; we help track the changing variables around land, cost, capital, draws, policy, incentives, and takeout so they can stay focused on the site. For first-time garden suite or multiplex owners, the same equation explains how we think before a shovel moves.

Accessibility Notes

  • Use a semantic section with aria-labelledby tied to the headline.
  • Cards should be real text content, not image-only labels.
  • The CTA must be a normal link with a clear accessible name: "Map my build financing."
  • If visual concepts introduce animated connectors or equation-like lines, they must be decorative with aria-hidden="true".
  • Maintain text contrast for lime accents by using lime as background/accent, not small body text on paper.

  • No guaranteed financing approval.
  • No guaranteed permits, zoning approvals, CMHC MLI Select qualification, or takeout approval.
  • No guaranteed project profitability, completion, timeline, contractor availability, or investor return.
  • No "free unlimited consulting" or broad professional-services promise.
  • No repetition of the existing "8-14% returns" claim.

Acceptance Criteria

  • The section is understandable as plain text before visual design.
  • The section has one job: explain FairLend's build-financing operating layer.
  • The CTA captures visitors from property, plan, and early-intent stages.
  • The differentiator is stronger than "complimentary" without promising unlimited services.
  • The transition into the builder equation is explicit and natural.
  • Unsupported claims and compliance-sensitive guarantees are absent.
