4. The Gap

The page moves from a menu of financing products directly into an animated equation about why single-family no longer pencils — without first establishing who FairLend is on a build, and why the visitor should trust FairLend (not just any lender) to help solve that equation. Builder Consulting opens mid-thought: a single row cycles across three years with no setup for why multiplex, what the variables mean, or what FairLend's role is beyond supplying money. The "density changes the outcome" landing is powerful but arrives with no runway.

Previous — Overview ends with

A six-card product menu (Residential Mortgages → MLI-Select) and the line "Disciplined capital. Local insight. Aligned outcomes."

Visitor belief: "FairLend funds a range of deals."

Next — Builder Consulting opens with

"Building shouldnt be the easy part" / "A successful build is not only a construction problem, it is a business equation." An equation board (LAND + BUILD COST + HOME PROGRAM = EXPECTED SALE → PROFIT) cycling years.

Visitor belief required: "FairLend understands these variables and will model mine."

7. Scope — "Full, With Progressive Disclosure"

LOCKED The section surfaces the full one-stop offering, but uses progressive disclosure so the headline scan stays light and detail is layered:

  1. Layer 0 — always-visible Headline + the end-to-end promise (property → financed multiplex → takeout) + primary CTA + the phase spine.
  2. Layer 1 — phase pillars Plan → Finance → Build support → Takeout. Four expandable anchors.
  3. Layer 2 — revealed detail Per pillar, the sub-services from the brief: site & feasibility, unit mix, budget pressure-test, capital structure; draw schedule, contractors/professionals network, permit strategy, CMHC MLI Select readiness, documentation; milestone/draw administration, site monitoring, recovery support; refinance, sale, rental stabilization, MLI Select takeout.
  4. Layer 3 — the "complimentary" anchor All advisory is complimentary when you finance with FairLend — surfaced as the positioning payoff, not buried.

8. Visual System (Preserve)

Grid & frame

FairlendLandingRail three-column wrapper (left gutter · content · right gutter), max-w-[1848px], cross-dots from 768px. The section is the frame — no floating page cards, no cards-in-cards.

Color allocation

Overview paper Builder warm paper Builder forest ink Builder coral (risk/mark) Blueprint blue (technical only) Action lime (one dominant action)

Tension to resolve in Phase 4: Overview is clean paper + serif; Builder Consulting is a feasibility board (cream, forest, coral, blueprint). The new section is the bridge between these two registers — likely warm paper + forest ink + serif positioning that earns the right to introduce phase pillars with restrained technical accents.

Typography

  • Big positioning: Cormorant Garamond serif (matches Overview "Who We Are").
  • Phase labels / technical accents: Oxanium mono uppercase, used sparingly (Label Budget Rule).
  • Body: Inter.
  • Condensed display (League Gothic) is reserved for Builder Consulting & Leadership — do not pull it into this bridge section or it steals Builder Consulting's register.

Components & motifs available

Numbered kicker (FairlendSectionKicker), corner-marker cards, ledger tabs, paper textures, skyline/route/topographic imagery, the builder-action-card pattern, the FinanceCard numbered-card pattern. Reuse over inventing.

Motion

GSAP scroll reveals via FairlendScrollChoreography; progressive disclosure implies expand/collapse or scroll-reveal of layer-2 detail; reduced-motion must flatten transforms.
