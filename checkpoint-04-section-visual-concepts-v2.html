Design rationale

Mirrors Overview's two-column editorial split (Who We Are | What We Finance) → now (Build Model | Operating Layer). Reads as the inevitable next page-spread, not a new chapter. Lowest register-disruption.

Grid & signature

1.05fr / 1fr split matches Overview. Signature: a single blueprint route-line spine runs down the pillar gutter, its nodes aligning to each pillar — visually rhymes with Overview's connector lines and hands into Builder Consulting's equation rail.

Type & color

Cormorant headline + Inter body + Oxanium pillar numbers (coral). Lime reserved for the single CTA. Blueprint blue appears only as the route-line stroke. No League Gothic. No grid fills.

Motion

Scroll-reveal of headline + pillars via FairlendScrollChoreography; route-line draws top-to-bottom on enter; chevron rotates on expand. All flatten under reduced-motion.

Responsive

Desktop: two columns. ≤900px: single column, pillars stack, spine becomes a short vertical accent above the pillars. CTA stays above the fold.

Accessibility

Native <details> accordions; chevron aria-hidden; CTA is a real link with visible focus ring; route-line is decorative aria-hidden SVG.

Risks & tradeoffs

Lowest-risk option — but the quietest. The route-line spine is the only signature element; if it under-delivers in build, the section may feel like "more of Overview" rather than a new beat. Mitigation: give the spine a coral node-fill on the active/first pillar.

Design rationale

A centered, full-width band that signals "we're shifting gears into build territory." The horizontal build-station track turns the four pillars into a process you read left-to-right — foreshadowing Builder Consulting's feasibility-board register without adopting its League Gothic. Copy treatment (user-selected refinement): the dense two-paragraph hero is broken into scannable units — an italic lead line, a coral variable ribbon (chips), a one-line shared lead, then two parallel audience cards (builders | first-timers). Same words, scan-first layout; section may exceed one viewport.

Grid & signature

Centered column for copy; 4-col station track below. Signature: each station carries a single-stroke blueprint plan/elevation line icon (site plan, loan stack, build form, takeout arrow), connected by a dashed coral route along the track tops.

Type & color

Same stack (Cormorant / Inter / Oxanium). Coral moves from a pure accent into the connector + active station — a controlled escalation that prepares the eye for Builder Consulting's coral-heavy risk marks. Lime stays single-use (CTA).

Motion

Stations cascade in left-to-right on scroll; coral route-line draws across the track; expanding a station slides its detail drawer down. Reduced-motion: instant.

Responsive

Desktop: 4-col track. ≤900px: stations stack vertically with the route-line bending 90° down the left edge — the process reads top-to-bottom on mobile.

Accessibility

Stations are <summary> toggles; active state conveyed by color + ring + aria-expanded (redundant). Icons are aria-hidden; meaning is in the text labels.

Risks & tradeoffs

Bolder than A — the centered layout is a register shift, which is the point, but risks feeling less "editorial FairLend." The station icons are the highest-effort art: if they read as generic, the concept loses its reason to exist. Mitigation: icons must be unmistakably blueprint linework, not icon-pack glyphs.

Design rationale

Leads with the strategic idea — build = an equation — as a single hand-drawn blueprint line drawing across the top. The pillars become annotated callouts pinned to it. This pre-figures Builder Consulting's equation board in blueprint language, so the handoff is the most inevitable of the three.

Grid & signature

Full-width master drawing on top; 1/1 split below (copy | pillars). Signature: the master build-equation drawing itself — property → plan → finance → build → takeout, in continuous single-stroke linework with a rotated "INCLUDED" plan-stamp.

Type & color

Oxanium labels live inside the drawing (node captions) — a bolder use of the technical register that still stays on warm paper + Cormorant headline. Coral carries the connectors; lime stays single-use. No League Gothic; no grid fills.

Motion

On scroll-into-view the master drawing strokes draw left-to-right (dashoffset), nodes pop, then the stamp rotates in. Pillars reveal after. Reduced-motion: static drawing, no draw-on.

Responsive

Master drawing scales full-width (it's an SVG). ≤900px: c-grid collapses; drawing stays but labels may abbreviate. Pillars remain accordion below.

Accessibility

Master drawing is role="img" with a descriptive aria-label (not aria-hidden in the final build — here shown aria-hidden as a render placeholder). Pillars are native <details>; stamp is decorative.

Risks & tradeoffs

The boldest, and the most effort to build well. The master drawing must read instantly as a build equation or it becomes decoration. Risk of crowding the Builder Consulting equation that follows — mitigation: this drawing is structural/schematic (blueprint linework), while Builder Consulting's is numeric/profit-led, so they rhyme without repeating.

Framework application ledger

CRAP (Contrast · Repetition · Alignment · Proximity)

Contrast via serif headline vs Oxanium labels; repetition of kicker/numbering system from Overview; alignment to the paper-rail grid; proximity groups each pillar with its sub-services. All three concepts.

Gestalt / Visual Hierarchy

A: spine continuity ties pillars into one figure. B: the station track is read as a single process (common region). C: the master drawing is the dominant figure; pillars are closure-attached callouts.

White Space / Grid Systems

Generous vertical rhythm preserved from Overview. A keeps the editorial split; B centralizes; C splits after a full-width figure.

Progressive Disclosure / Hick's

Accordion pillars in all three limit first-scan to 4 choices (within 7±2); sub-services revealed on demand — directly drove the per-concept pillar treatment.

Brand Personality / Distinctive Assets

Reused: warm paper, Cormorant, Oxanium, lime-one-action, blueprint linework. Avoided AI-slop: no grid/repeating fills, no black-bg acid-green, no League Gothic in this section.

Fitts's Law

CTA sized as a large tap target with generous padding; station/pillar summaries are full-width tap rows on mobile — drove the mobile station-stack in B and pillar treatment in A/C.
