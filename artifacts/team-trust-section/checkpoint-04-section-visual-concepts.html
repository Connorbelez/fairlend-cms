Grid

Oversized thesis followed by four vertical title-block columns linked by one lime route.

Motion

The route may draw once left-to-right over 600ms. Reduced motion shows the finished line immediately.

Tradeoff

Highest recall and strongest widescreen silhouette; mobile must collapse to a vertical line without resembling process steps.

Shared systemLocked across all concepts
TypographyExisting Cormorant/Inter/Oxanium registers; headline tracking no tighter than -0.04em; 16px minimum body.
ColorPaper dominant; forest structural; lime ≤2% and only accountability/verification.
ComponentsSemantic list/articles and ruled register—not team cards, tabs, portraits, or hidden disclosures.
ResponsiveDesktop coordinates rows/columns; tablet reduces metadata; mobile preserves name → role → discipline → responsibility.
Accessibility4.5:1 text contrast, semantic headings, decorative routes ignored, default-visible content, reduced-motion equivalent.
AssetsNo new raster assets required. Existing paper texture only; headshots intentionally deferred until a complete consistent set exists.
