Impeccable shape brief

  • Composition: one content-height, native-scroll continuation of Leadership; a strong proposition on the left and a ruled four-person operating roster on the right or below, responsive by composition rather than shrinkage.
  • Visual lane: “Toronto financing field guide”—a deal-file responsibility ledger, not editorial-magazine affectation and not SaaS team cards.
  • Information model: name + current role + discipline + one concise client-value responsibility + one substantiated credential. No social links, hobbies, or long bios.
  • Motion: default content remains visible; optional one-time rule-draw or dossier-settle entrance only. No pin, scrub, carousel, counter, tab, or accordion.
  • Identity preservation: existing Cormorant/Inter/Oxanium voices, ivory/ink/forest/lime palette, groovepaper material, hard rules, and current leadership primitives.

Risk controls

  • Too long: it becomes an About page and delays the FAQ. Cap each person to one responsibility and one proof.
  • Too vague: titles become prestige labels with no client meaning. Every entry must translate role into file-level value.
  • Too visually loud: it competes with the equation and founder reveal. Keep it static, unnumbered, and materially continuous.
  • Too portrait-led without consistent assets: uneven headshots reduce trust. Use a text-led ledger unless the complete set meets one art direction.
  • Too claim-heavy: duplicated volume, tenure, and “top broker” claims create compliance and credibility risk. Let the founder panel own its approved proof.
  • CTA competition: a new action fractures the trust sequence. Keep the existing Leadership consultation CTA as the chapter’s sole action.

Acceptance criteria

  1. A visitor can name at least three distinct disciplines behind a FairLend file after scanning the section.
  2. The section demonstrates execution depth without reading as an About-page biography grid.
  3. It feels like the conclusion of Leadership, not an additional numbered section.
  4. Native scrolling resumes immediately after the pinned Leadership reveal.
  5. No new CTA or interaction competes with the existing booking action.
  6. Every visible credential and responsibility is substantiated or explicitly withheld pending confirmation.
  7. The section composes at mobile, tablet, and desktop without requiring horizontal scrolling or hiding core content.
