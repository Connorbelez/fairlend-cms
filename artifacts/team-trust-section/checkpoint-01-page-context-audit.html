The page moves from corporate identity, to build capability, to an economic thesis, to founder-led credibility. It then jumps directly into FAQ. The missing belief is not “FairLend has employees.” It is: the expertise shown in the leadership reveal is backed by named operators who own distinct parts of a complex file.

Insertion diagnosis

Previous section

Leadership reveal

A founder-led 100svh trust panel with principal-broker proof, capabilities, commitments, and a booking CTA. On desktop it is pinned for approximately 145% additional viewport scroll.

Next section

FAQ

Objection handling across borrower, builder, investor, and partner concerns. It is information-dense and should follow a calmer proof beat.

Narrative gap

“Team” is asserted, not demonstrated

The leadership headline says the equation still needs a team and the summary says “meet the people,” but the visible experience is primarily one founder portrait.

Conversion gap

Execution ownership is unclear

A serious borrower, builder, partner, or investor can see senior judgment but cannot yet answer who handles technology, operations, administration, or the operating lifecycle around the file.

What the new trust beat should accomplish

  • Build belief that FairLend has an accountable, multidisciplinary operating bench—not a founder plus a generic support team.
  • Translate each person into client value using “what they own in your file”, not biography-first copy.
  • Connect mortgage judgment, capital-markets/technology depth, and operating discipline to the financing lifecycle already explained above.
  • Reduce key-person risk without diluting the founder’s authority.
  • Create a calm, native-scroll decompression beat between two pinned sections and the FAQ.

Working content thesis for Phase 2: “Know who owns the hard parts.” This is a directional strategy line, not locked copy.

CTA hierarchy

Constraints and open decisions

  1. Roster: confirm which people belong on the homepage and their current public-facing titles.
  2. Ownership: confirm the concrete client/file responsibility each person can credibly claim.
  3. Portraits: supply consistent headshots for the approved roster, or explicitly choose a text-led dossier treatment.
  4. CTA placement: retain the current Leadership CTA or move it to close the combined Leadership + operating bench chapter.
  5. External bench: decide whether legal, recovery, appraisal, and construction specialists are shown as named people, capability labels, or omitted.
