Framework Application Ledger

What changed because of the design frameworks.

CRAP

Contrast separates CTA and thesis moments; repetition preserves FairLend rails; alignment keeps service variables scannable; proximity groups the equation pieces.

Gestalt

Concepts group land, capital, readiness, and exit as one operating layer before the equation section.

Visual Hierarchy

Each concept keeps the headline first, CTA second, service map third, transition thesis last.

Grid Systems

Dense four-card, accordion, and 12-column layouts create distinct directions without empty grid cells.

White Space

Large vertical sections preserve the cinematic FairLend page rhythm and avoid crowding the builder equation.

Fitts's Law

The CTA remains a large, high-contrast target in every concept.

Progressive Disclosure

Mobile order prioritizes headline, CTA, four rows, differentiator, then transition thesis.

Brand Personality

All concepts preserve technical, local-market, expert-operator energy rather than generic one-stop-shop marketing.
