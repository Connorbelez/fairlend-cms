Checkpoint 02 / Section Strategy Lock

Lock the section as FairLend's build-partner operating layer.

The new section should make one strategic move: after visitors see what FairLend finances, show them that construction financing with FairLend comes with the planning intelligence, coordination, and end-to-end build support needed to make the project equation work.

Page Context Summary

Page

FairLend homepage at src/app/(frontend)/page.tsx.

Insertion Point

Between FairlendLandingOverviewSection and FairlendBuilderConsultingSection.

Previous Checkpoint

checkpoint-01-page-context-audit.html approved with Phase 2 notes.

Recommended Section Strategy

Dominant Role

Offer Explainer.

Secondary Roles

Bridge Section, Mechanism Section, Objection Handler, CTA Preparation Section.

Insertion Thesis

The page currently moves from broad financing categories to build economics too quickly. Visitors need to understand FairLend's build-partner model before the multiplex equation can land as a reason to work with FairLend.

Strategic Frame

Not "extra consulting." Not "free advice." The frame is an included operating advantage: when you finance with FairLend, the build planning intelligence travels with the capital.

Locked Belief

Target visitor belief

"I can come to FairLend with a property, a plan, or even early build intent, and they can help me shape the financing, project strategy, professional path, draw plan, permit/MLI readiness, and takeout direction in one place."

Visitor Question + Objection

Primary Question Answered

"If I want to build or convert a property, does FairLend only provide the loan, or can they help me figure out the whole path?"

Primary Objection Addressed

"I do not know whether I have the right property, plan, team, capital stack, draw logic, permit strategy, MLI path, or exit financing yet."

Core Message

FairLend is the build-financing partner that helps turn a property, plan, or early intent into a structured project equation: land, scope, team, permits, capital, draws, execution, and takeout.

Supporting Points

  1. Start from any stage. The section should explicitly capture visitors who have only a property, an acquisition target, a rough plan, or early intent.
  2. Build the equation before the build hardens. FairLend helps evaluate land basis, unit strategy, construction budget, regulatory path, capital structure, draw planning, and takeout direction.
  3. Bring the project network closer. FairLend can connect clients with contractors, consultants, professionals, suppliers, financing partners, and mortgage capital sources where appropriate.
  4. Make the included advisory layer a differentiator. The section should position build planning and financing strategy as a major reason to finance with FairLend, not as a small bonus or generic complimentary service.

Proof Requirements

  • Use Company Brief support for construction financing specialization and build advisory layer.
  • Use existing page support for construction financing, draw schedules, CMHC MLI Select availability language, and professional network access.
  • Use careful capability language: "helps," "supports," "can connect," "prepare for," and "structure around."
  • Do not use unsupported success rates, approval rates, project returns, timelines, or qualification promises.
  • Do not repeat or amplify the existing "8-14% returns" claim in this section.

CTA Strategy

Behavior

Single dominant lime CTA: "Book a free consultation" → /intake?intent=consultation&source=build-model-consultation.

Audience Captured

Property owners, buyers, builders, and developers with a property, a plan, or early build intent.

CTA Job

Start a build conversation before the visitor has every answer. The CTA should lower the threshold to engage, not imply they must already have permit drawings, a contractor, and a completed capital plan. "Free consultation" signals the advisory layer is accessible at the very first step.

One-Action Rule (locked)

This is the only lime action in the section. No secondary CTAs. Builder Consulting keeps its own "RUN MY NUMBERS" CTA downstream — the two do not compete (partnership vs. equation).

Section Identity & Build Contract (locked this session)

Kicker

03 / Our Build Model

Progressive Disclosure

Accordion / disclosure pattern (tap-to-expand pillars). Chosen for mobile: thumb-friendly, native disclosure semantics, keyboard + screen-reader accessible, no hover dependency. Hover-reveal rejected (no touch equivalent).

Disclosure Layers

Layer 0 (always visible): headline + end-to-end promise + CTA + four-pillar spine.
Layer 1: pillar headers (Plan / Finance / Build support / Takeout).
Layer 2: per-pillar sub-services revealed on expand.
Layer 3: "complimentary when you finance with FairLend" as the closing payoff.

Rail Texture

fabric-of-squares — bookends the hero; reads as a brand-level model statement, distinct from both adjacent bands.

Imagery Constraint

Blueprint LINE motifs only (SVG plan/elevation linework, route/path lines, corner markers, single-stroke technical drawing accents). No grid-pattern or repeating-background fills anywhere — those read as AI slop. Decorative tiling is prohibited.

Register Bridge

Warm paper + forest ink + Cormorant serif positioning (Overview register) that earns the right to introduce restrained Oxanium technical accents and blueprint line motifs, handing into Builder Consulting's feasibility board. League Gothic stays OUT of this section.

Transition Logic

Previous Section Leaves Visitor Thinking

FairLend finances multiple types of real estate projects and has local, disciplined expertise.

New Section Explains

For build-related projects, FairLend's value is the integrated planning and financing layer around the project.

Next Section Can Then Show

Why the project equation matters, how old assumptions broke, and why density can change the outcome.

Desired Effects

Emotional Effect

Relief and confidence: FairLend can help make the path legible before the client has assembled every part of the build.

Cognitive Effect

The visitor understands the build as a multi-variable equation and FairLend as the team that helps structure those variables around financing.

Risks To Avoid

  • Too long: the section becomes a second services page and delays the equation.
  • Too vague: "end-to-end partner" reads like generic agency copy and fails to explain the actual services.
  • Too visually loud: it competes with the builder equation instead of handing into it.
  • Too many CTAs: the visitor faces another route-selector moment. Use one build-specific action.
  • Too promissory: wording implies guaranteed permits, CMHC qualification, financing approval, completion, exit, or profit.

Acceptance Criteria

  • The section clearly says FairLend helps with the project equation, not just the financing product.
  • The section welcomes property, plan, and early-intent visitors.
  • The section names the service categories without turning into an exhaustive process manual.
  • The CTA is build-specific and does not compete with unrelated investor or mortgage routes.
  • The included advisory/planning layer is framed as a core differentiator of financing with FairLend.
  • The handoff into the 2019/2023/2026 equation feels inevitable.
