# Partner Program Page — Section-by-Section Breakdown

**Page:** FairLend Partner Program
**Audience:** Mortgage brokers, real-estate agents, architects, planners, engineers, builders, project managers, consultants, trades, suppliers — the GTA build ecosystem
**Goal:** Educate potential partners on the program, establish FairLend as an end-to-end build financing and execution partner, reassure brokers the client relationship stays theirs, and convert via `Apply to Become a Partner` / `Bring Us a Project`
**Visual direction:** Editorial Topographic Grid system (per `DESIGN.md`) — paper/ink palette, electric lime action cues, serif display type, visible grid and gutter lines, topographic contour motifs, pen-and-ink Toronto illustration
**Source:** `docs/context/partner-program-page-content-plan.md`

> **Compliance note (applies throughout):** $2B+ funded, "nearly three decades," DrawFlow capabilities, and any CMHC MLI Select language are flagged inline `[COMPLIANCE]` and must be substantiated before publishing. Never publish guaranteed approval, funding, qualification, completion, timeline, cost, or exit. Compensation/referral terms are discussed at onboarding, never printed as promises. Broker non-circumvention wording must be confirmed with legal before it hardens beyond the operating-principle framing used below.

> **Frame guardrail (applies throughout):** FairLend is never positioned as a late-stage "financeability checker." Every section reinforces: FairLend helps *create* the equation — site, plan, capital, draws, exit — before the project hardens.

## Cross-Section Notes

- **CTA discipline:** Only approved CTA language appears — Apply to Become a Partner, Bring Us a Project, Talk Through a Client Scenario, Submit a Site or Build Opportunity, Book a Partner Fit Call. Never "Submit," "Learn More," "Contact Us," "Get Started."
- **Dark bands:** Use at most two dark-ink sections per assembled page (candidates: 7C, 14C). If both are selected, reconsider one.
- **Broker promise cadence:** "Your client remains your client" should appear at minimum in the hero, Section 6, and Section 14 — regardless of concepts chosen.
- **Forbidden imagery:** No handshake photography, no stock hard-hat photos, no referral-badge aesthetics, no cartoon construction diagrams, no three-card "brokers/agents/architects" audience layout.
- **Tone words to favor:** structure, sequence, package, administer, draw, milestone, takeout, working capital, execution, site strategy, build equation.
- **Claims requiring substantiation before launch:** $2B+ funded; nearly three decades; any ranking claim; DrawFlow described as active capability; SLA language; compensation/referral terms; all CMHC MLI Select statements; legal/recovery team composition.
