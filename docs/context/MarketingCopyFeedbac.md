- “Call us” / “find out how” CTA while detailed partner pages are still being built.

### 8.3 Co-Broker / Broker Messaging

Broker pain points:
- They want to close construction deals.
- They do not want to lose their client.
- They need help explaining and structuring construction financing.
- They need confidence that FairLend will support, not replace, their client relationship.

Important copy decisions:
- Use benefit language, not feature language.
- Say “construction advisory team” or “construction financing advisory team,” not “tech team.”
- Make it clear that the partner’s client remains their client.
- Promise help winning deals, not just access to software.

Recommended headline:
- `Our construction advisory team helps you win construction deals`

Supporting bullets:
- `Your clients remain your clients`
- `We meet with you and your client to structure the plan`
- `Co-broker construction financing with FairLend`
- `Use DrawFlow to explain the build, budget, and draw path`
- `Help your clients move from idea to funded project`

CTA:
- `Find out how`
- `Book a partner presentation`
- `Request partner information`

### 8.4 Real Estate Agent Messaging

The real estate agent value proposition needs more refinement.

Core insight:
- Many agents have builder, flipper, and investor clients who stopped buying because single-family flips no longer pencil the way they used to.
- FairLend can help these clients re-enter the market through multiplex / construction financing solutions.

Potential copy directions:
- `Bring back your builders and flippers`
- `Help your investor clients find buildable opportunities again`
- `Turn stalled investor demand into construction-ready deals`
- `Give your builder clients a financing path for multiplex projects`

### 8.5 Avoid “Formula” Language

**Decision:** Avoid using `formula` in partner-facing or realtor-facing copy.

Reason:
- It sounds complicated.
- It may make people feel like they need to do work or understand a complex model before engaging.

Preferred:
- `solution`
- `system`
- `path`
- `process`
- `model`

Use carefully:
- `multiplex solution`
- `construction financing solution`
- `DrawFlow system`
