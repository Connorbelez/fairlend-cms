# Builder Consulting illustrative assumptions

Snapshot: 2026-07-13  
Geography: Southern Ontario, with Toronto-specific examples retained where zoning or the reviewed scenario requires them.

These are partner-review-approved explanatory assumptions from the website review meeting. They are not an appraisal, market report, investment forecast, profit projection, or financing commitment. Before publication of new market claims, replace or validate each assumption against an approved dated source.

## Model conventions

- Luxury infill (2019 illustration): $1.00M land basis, 3,800 ft², $400/ft² hard cost, $50/ft² soft-cost allowance including financing, and a $3.65M illustrative exit.
- Later single-family states: hard costs and soft costs are independent inputs. Both are included exactly once in total cost.
- Multiplex rental: 5 units, $1.10M land basis, 7,500 ft², $250/ft² hard cost, $50/ft² soft cost, $4,000 monthly rent per unit, and a 35% operating-expense allowance.
- Rental valuation: 5% illustrative capitalization rate.
- Garden suite: one-home output and a $400K–$600K total build-cost range. The displayed soft-cost field says `included` so those costs are not counted twice.
- Cash yield: annual cash flow divided by cash invested. The calculation supports positive, zero, and negative cash flow and never converts a nonzero cash flow to `0.0%` because of a unit mismatch.
