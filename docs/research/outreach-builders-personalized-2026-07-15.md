# FairLend builder and early-file professional outreach dossiers

**Prepared:** 2026-07-15  
**Scope:** 20 Ontario builders, design-build firms, permit designers, and engineers that encounter construction files before capital structure and draw mechanics are fixed.  
**Method:** First-party company pages, portfolios, team biographies, published articles, and official company social content were used wherever available. Public facts are cited; financing diagnoses are explicitly FairLend inferences, not claims about a lead's existing clients. No private email, guessed name, or unverified project is included.

## How to use this file

- Personalization in the scripts is grounded in the cited intelligence and hooks. Remove the research links when sending unless the link itself is the value-add.
- Replace `{{sender}}`, `{{sender title}}`, `{{recipient first name}}`, and scheduling placeholders before use.
- The recommended first ask is deliberately small: a workflow conversation or a redacted-file dry run, never an immediate referral commitment.
- DrawFlow must be described as a **milestone-based draw workflow**: as approved milestones are completed and verified, draw availability *can* unlock. It is not guaranteed funding, guaranteed timing, or a promise to eliminate interest or contractor-payment gaps.
- Any live file requires client consent. Engineering/design independence, borrower suitability, underwriting, valuation, legal review, file completeness, available capital, and project economics remain separate.

## Shared offer architecture

1. **Early capital-readiness review:** site/control, project type, scope, permit path, budget, borrower equity/liquidity, contingency, schedule, valuation support, and exit.
2. **Permit-to-capital handoff:** a partner-specific intake that reuses the plans, estimates, schedules, and consultant outputs the partner already produces.
3. **DrawFlow mapping:** convert an agreed construction budget and sequence into proposed evidence and milestone groups for lender review.
4. **Execution support:** document gaps, progress evidence, draw administration, escalation, and recovery resources where applicable.
5. **Exit planning:** refinance, sale, rental stabilization, or insured takeout where available and appropriate.

Pause the sequence on any reply. Route objections to a human; do not auto-send rebuttals. For professional firms, secure consent and role boundaries before introducing compensation. For builders with current financing/software relationships, lead with complement/overflow/interoperability.
