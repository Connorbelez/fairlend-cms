# Partner Lead company and people verification

Verified 2026-07-15. This is the entity-resolution companion to the 100-lead partnership corpus and the four personalized outreach dossiers. It is intentionally conservative: a named person is considered CRM-ready only when a first-party organization page, official show/feed/program material, or the person's public professional profile establishes both identity and role. Organization inboxes are preserved as company routes and are never converted into invented personal emails.

## Confidence and normalization rules

- **High:** current official team, contact, program, speaker, host, or organization page directly identifies the person and role.
- **Medium:** an official feed, event page, or organizer profile identifies the person and role, but no current direct business contact is published.
- **Manual hold:** a partial name, old organizer attribution, or profile without a verified current role. Do not create or link a Person until reverified.
- Company names below are legal names only where the primary source publishes them; otherwise they are the canonical operating identity. An unincorporated Meetup/community is explicitly marked as a company exception.
- “No direct email/phone” means none was located in the reviewed first-party material; it does not authorize enrichment from guessed email patterns or data brokers.
