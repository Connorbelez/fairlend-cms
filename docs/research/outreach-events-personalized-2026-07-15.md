# FairLend event-partnership outreach dossiers

**Research date:** July 15, 2026  
**Operating premise:** FairLend should buy access only when it receives a credible educational surface, curated meetings, or a measurable referral pathway. A logo-only booth is not the default. “DrawFlow” is framed as the operating discipline around construction-file readiness, milestone-based draws, evidence, and communication—not as a promise of approval, speed, rate, or proceeds.

## Channel sequence used for every event

1. Email the named sponsorship/program owner with a specific activation, not “send your deck.”
2. Same day: LinkedIn connection note referencing one agenda item.
3. Day 3: short contact-form version if email is unacknowledged.
4. Day 6: call with a two-minute qualification agenda.
5. Day 10: send a one-page mock activation.
6. Day 17: commercial follow-up with success metrics and a small-pilot option.
7. Day 24: close the loop and set a re-contact date.

## Event portfolio rules

- **Priority 1:** OHBA, Missing Middle/Developers Den, Vision, The Buildings Show and BUZZ. They combine early-file access with a plausible measurable activation.
- **Priority 2:** PM Springfest, OAA, AIC, CCE and WIMS. Strong education/partner value, but role boundaries matter.
- **Priority 3:** Land & Development, CAIC, RealCapital, Toronto REF, Canadian REAM and CLS. Pursue for channel partners or standards—not mass borrower acquisition.
- **Conditional:** CHBA only if the Ontario-only service boundary is accepted; OBOA only as neutral education; BILD only if the hospitality package enables real conversations.
- Require, in writing: content surface, attendee selection or opt-in mechanics, follow-up permissions, expected audience, total cost and cancellation terms.
- **Go/no-go economics:** do not sponsor unless the event can plausibly create at least 8 qualified partner conversations or 5 qualified project office hours. Track cost per qualified conversation, accepted follow-up, partner pilot, project submitted and funded project; never use badge scans as the success metric.
