## Outreach guardrails

Every pitch must lead with the checklist, worksheet, case or diagnostic—not FairLend or DrawFlow. DrawFlow appears only as an illustrative operating method after the educational framework. Do not promise approvals, rates, savings, returns or project outcomes. For audiences outside Ontario, distinguish universal project controls from Ontario-specific licensing and lending examples. Where the audience requires a technical credential, pair Elie with an architect, engineer, PQS, lawyer, lender-risk professional, REALTOR or local broker.
