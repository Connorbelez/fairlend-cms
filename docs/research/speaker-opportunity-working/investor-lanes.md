- Stable IDs run contiguously from `SPEAKER-2026-001` through `SPEAKER-2026-035`.
- Opportunity-series and event-occurrence keys are unique. Repeated organizations below are intentional `EXISTING_ORGANIZATION_NEW_PROGRAM` records for materially distinct programs, not duplicate organization leads.
- Dedupe scope included the July 15 research corpus, source-controlled partner-lead manifests, and the complete read-only Twenty snapshot of 181 Partner Leads supplied during this run.
- Timing is conservative: a blank next date means the official source proves an active recurring program but does not publish the next occurrence. `ONGOING_CONFIRM_OPEN` means the program is live but the next speaker opening requires organizer confirmation.

Speaking-access classes used here:

- `A_OPEN_APPLICATION`: explicit speaker, proposal, or education-partner mechanism.
- `B_PROGRAM_CONTACT`: a named programmer or official programming inbox is published.
- `C_PROVEN_GUEST_FORMAT`: current or recent external experts prove the format, with an official route available.
- `D_SPONSOR_EDUCATION`: credible education access exists through partnership inventory; editorial fit must be attempted first.
- `E_ORGANIZER_ROUTE_ONLY`: active education and an official organizer route are verified, but no public speaker mechanism is published.

## Operational cautions

- Elie’s opening framing should always be the audience’s construction-capital problem, never “FairLend wants to present.”
- The educational artifact comes first: a draw-readiness scorecard, file audit, capital-stack stress test, or milestone worksheet. DrawFlow is the optional operating demonstration after the framework is taught.
- For Quebec opportunities, use French-first materials and a Quebec co-presenter. For BC, Alberta, Saskatchewan, and Nova Scotia, keep legal and lender-specific statements jurisdiction-neutral or use a local co-panelist.
- For CREW chapters, preserve the women-advancement mandate by using a woman leader as the primary project storyteller and Elie as a supporting technical expert.
- Paid or sponsor-linked access is not authorization to deliver a product pitch. Attempt editorial placement first and document content independence before purchasing inventory.
- Dates, agendas, and speaker inventory can change. Re-verify immediately before outreach; never infer an email address or scrape personal contact data.
