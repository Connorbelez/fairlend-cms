# FairLend partnership prospecting brief

**Research date:** 2026-07-15  
**Primary geography:** GTA and Southern Ontario first; Ontario and Canada-wide channels where the audience or leverage justifies it  
**Scope:** 100 qualified partnership leads — 20 builders/early-file professionals, 20 podcasts, 20 investor groups, 20 meetups/recurring communities, and 20 trade shows/events

## Executive recommendation

FairLend should not lead with “send us mortgage leads.” The stronger partner proposition is:

> Bring FairLend in before the project hardens. We help structure the site, scope, budget, capital, milestone draws, working capital, professional path and takeout around one financeable project.

DrawFlow is the sharpest entry point because it solves a visible operating problem: completed work, supporting evidence, lender review and capital release are often disconnected. The partnership offer should protect the originating relationship, use consented introductions, define roles early and avoid approval, timeline, savings or outcome guarantees.

## Qualification method

Leads were ranked within each category using five factors:

1. **Early-file or audience access (30%)** — how early the organization sees acquisition, design, permit, budget, renovation or development intent.
2. **DrawFlow/service relevance (25%)** — whether FairLend’s feasibility, private/construction financing, milestone draws, working-capital planning, build support and takeout planning solve an observable need.
3. **Partnership openness (20%)** — official evidence of guest experts, sponsors, vendors, professional networks, referrals, collaboration, member benefits or partner programs.
4. **Geographic relevance (15%)** — GTA/Southern Ontario first, then Ontario and high-leverage Canadian channels.
5. **Reachability (10%)** — a live public organizer, programming, sponsor, contact or inquiry route.

Ranks are strategic priorities, not claims that a partnership will close. Lower-ranked rows with older public programming are explicitly marked for re-verification before any spend.

### Recommended cadence per lead

- **Day 0:** Personalized email or organizer DM referencing the specific evidence in the lead row.
- **Day 3:** Send the one useful asset promised in the pitch; do not send a generic capabilities deck.
- **Day 7:** Follow up with two concrete meeting times and the proposed session/pilot title.
- **Day 14:** Close the loop politely with one final relevant insight or anonymized project scenario.
- **After interest:** Run a 20-minute fit call covering audience, eligible files, role protection, consent, communication, compliance and success measures.

## Partner guardrails

- Obtain client consent before any file or personally identifiable information is shared.
- Define who owns the relationship, who communicates what, and how status updates work.
- Do not promise approval, funding, draw timing, interest savings, CMHC qualification, completion, refinance or investment outcomes.
- Confirm any referral compensation, co-marketing, sponsorship, exclusivity and non-circumvention terms with FairLend legal/compliance before offering them.
- Keep educational content useful with any lender; the partnership earns trust through operating quality, not pressure.
- For dormant or stale-event leads, verify 2026 programming before spending money or assigning senior time.
