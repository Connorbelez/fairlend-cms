06

Cannibalization contract

01

Residential vs A/institutional

Residential owns broad home-mortgage education and service intent; A/institutional owns prime lender qualification and bank-grade placement.

02

A vs B vs private

One comparison page owns the matrix; A and B are institutional tiers, while private is equity/exit-led. Each service page owns only its tier.

03

Private vs bank-declined

The existing private service owns commercial and urgent financing. The decline guide diagnoses and routes; it must not become a second private-lender service page.

04

HELOC vs refinance vs renovation

One comparison page owns cross-option trade-offs. HELOC owns revolving equity, refinance replaces a first mortgage, renovation owns project-specific outcomes.

05

Bridge vs private

Bridge owns a temporary proceeds/closing gap. “No firm sale” is a private bridge variant within the bridge owner, not a new generic private page.

06

Renovation vs construction

Renovation owns improvements to an existing dwelling. Ground-up/major structural progress draws remain with construction financing.

07

Guide vs service vs rescue

Guides educate, comparisons decide, services qualify/convert, rescue triages urgency. Only the canonical service page makes the full commercial pitch.

08

Ontario vs Toronto/GTA

Ontario is canonical by default. Add Toronto/GTA sections/examples only when lender policy, property type, timelines, or observed demand materially differs; do not clone city pages.
