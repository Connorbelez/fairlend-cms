POSITION

The plan is a portfolio, not a publishing queue.

The research supports a coherent information architecture. Google’s first-party evidence is too young to justify treating all proposed pages as proven demand, so execution must separate validated actions, bounded experiments, historical priorities, and directional hypotheses.

Concentrate authority before multiplying URLs.

Update the pages Google already recognizes, publish only two tightly bounded guides, instrument conversion outcomes, and promote the remaining clusters only when topic, page ownership, and user behaviour agree.

  • Protect existing canonical owners.
  • Make service pages conversion-capable.
  • Use guides to answer pre-qualification questions.
  • Keep audience boundaries explicit.
  • Link every guide to one commercial owner.
  • Measure outcomes, not traffic alone.
Ship / update nowPrivate mortgage, construction, investor, garden-suite, and partner pages; two bounded guides.
Prove before splittingGarden-suite financing and private-mortgage cost intent should strengthen current owners first.
Do not mislabel hypothesesTwenty-one clusters remain strategically useful but have no current FairLend Google signal.

FairLend should not try to look large by publishing 32 thin destinations. It should look authoritative by making the first seven destinations materially better than anything currently ranking.

Vertical links

Guide ↔ owner

Pair each guide and service page with exact-entity anchors. Tools link forward to the relevant service; service pages link back to deeper explanations.

Lateral links

Adjacent decisions

HELOC ↔ refinance ↔ renovation; construction ↔ multiplex ↔ garden ↔ MLI; acquisition ↔ refinance. Comparison pages may link to both, but only one owns the comparison query.

Audience firewall

Borrower ≠ investor ≠ partner

Use distinct schema, CTA language, navigation labels, and anchor text. Reciprocal links must name the audience and purpose explicitly.

Canonical rules

Preserve /borrowers/institutional-mortgage; keep /start/builder noindex as an intake endpoint; let construction own draw mechanics; let property-program pages own feasibility; let MLI own program requirements; split rental acquisition and refinance by transaction state.

Definition of done

Unique intent, one canonical owner, reviewed title/meta/H1, complete evidence blocks, correct schema, contextual internal links, working CTA, GA4 events, GSC baseline, and named refresh owner.

Promotion rule

Move a cluster from directional to validated only when the topic appears, the intended page owns it, and post-click behaviour is consistent with the page’s job.

Consolidation rule

If two URLs split the same query family or one page remains absent while another ranks, merge content into the recognized owner and redirect or hold the weaker route.

Visibility

Can Google identify the owner?

Non-brand impressions, query families, indexed pages, position distribution, CTR, guide-versus-service ownership, and cannibalization.

Behaviour

Does the page satisfy its job?

Engaged sessions, engagement time, scroll/content interactions, tool completion, guide-to-service click-through, and return visits.

Commercial

Does organic create qualified action?

Application starts/submits, bookings, referrals, investor interest, qualified-lead rate, assisted conversion, and eventual funded/referred outcome where privacy permits.

Privacy + data quality

Do not send loan amounts, property addresses, names, credit details, or free-form form values to GA4. Use controlled categorical parameters and reconcile qualified outcomes in the appropriate CRM or back-office system.

GOVERN

Make evidence freshness part of the content system.

Financial content can age quickly. Every claim, calculation, program rule, rate illustration, fee range, and eligibility statement needs a source, review owner, and refresh trigger.

Pre-publication gate

  1. Confirm search intent and canonical owner.
  2. Verify primary sources and effective dates.
  3. Separate facts, examples, assumptions, and FairLend opinion.
  4. Review financial, regulatory, privacy, and accessibility risk.
  5. Validate title/H1/snippet, schema, canonicals, and internal links.
  6. Verify CTA routing and event collection.
  7. Record baseline and 28-day review date.

Known limitations

  1. Only 67 direct GSC impressions exist in the current capture.
  2. Most proposed pages do not exist and cannot yet own queries.
  3. Combined GSC query,page extraction is not automated locally.
  4. GA4 records zero key events, so revenue contribution is unknown.
  5. Representative SERPs do not prove pairwise cluster overlap.
  6. Answer Socrates is discovery evidence, not search-volume validation.

Review monthly

Indexation, query ownership, CTR, striking-distance terms, organic landing behaviour, key-event rate, and competing URLs.

Review on change

CMHC/program rules, regulator guidance, municipal policy, product criteria, underwriting model, and site route changes.

Decision record

For every new URL: owner query, excluded queries, canonical relationship, required proof, reviewer, KPI, and consolidation trigger.
