AI discoverability starts with answerable decisions

The site has useful technical AI signals, but not enough original, reviewed content for reliable citation. The trial modeled thirty decision questions without pretending they have search volume.

Answer-engine strategy

Publish evidence units, not “AI SEO” filler.

Each answer should state the decision, define the boundary, cite a current authority, expose FairLend's original expertise, and route the reader to the correct qualification step.

  • Lead with the direct answer in a passage-sized block.
  • Separate program facts from FairLend product claims.
  • Show author, reviewer, update date, and source trail.
  • Pair text with matrices, checklists, calculators, or diagrams.
Nine-surface reality check
  • Modeled: 30 ChatGPT-style decision prompts; no volume claims.
  • Qualitative only: Reddit language around deposits and GTA projects.
  • Partial: YouTube appeared as a domain, without video evidence.
  • Not exposed: PAA, related searches, and AI Overview payloads.
  • Not collected: autocomplete, independent forums, and Bing/Copilot.

What the trial could not prove about AI visibility

What can be said—and what stays locked

Financial-services SEO is a proof system. Seven claims have authoritative support for brief requirements; six remain prohibited, review-only, or provisional. None bypasses final compliance review.

Supported for brief requirements
  • MLI Select applies to projects with at least five residential units and a qualifying point score.Current official program source; refresh before publication.
  • Specified point levels may permit up to 95% LTC and up to 50-year amortization for qualifying new construction.Present conditions and trade-offs, never a blanket promise.
  • Toronto fiveplex and sixplex permissions require address-specific zoning review.Do not imply citywide permission.
  • CMHC Refinance for densification up to four units is distinct from MLI Select.Keep the two-to-four vs. five-plus boundary explicit.
  • Garden and laneway suites are separate planning concepts.Use one financing route, but preserve the permit/site differences.
  • Toronto short-term rental rules constrain ancillary-suite use.Keep rental strategy current and sourced.
  • Ontario referral arrangements require role, compensation, conflict, consent, and information-use review.FSRA and RECO controls belong in the workflow.
Locked, prohibited, or review-only
  • Do not advertise the proposed federal CAD 80,000 secondary-suite loan as available.Status: prohibited.
  • Do not advertise Toronto's former Affordable Laneway Suites forgivable loan as current.Status: prohibited.
  • The announced enhanced rental rebate needs current availability and project-eligibility review.Status: research-only.
  • DrawFlow's line-of-credit-like mechanism needs product documents and operations approval.Status: provisional-unverified.
  • “Up to 50% interest saved” needs the case template, comparison assumptions, and deliberate approval.Status: provisional-unverified.
  • “One-stop shop” or end-to-end partner positioning needs named roles, boundaries, agreements, and disclaimers.Status: provisional-unverified.
Material limitations that change how to interpret the findings
Keyword metrics are mostly Canada-level. In the first trial, the paid four-query Toronto SERP batch failed after a USD 0.014 charge and one retry retained a USD 0.02 cost commitment. The expansion then persisted three successful single-query Toronto SERPs, while 29 of 102 exact metric rows still returned unavailable volume. No GSC, Bing Webmaster Tools, GA4, CRM, GBP, backlink graph, rank history, or field Core Web Vitals data was available. B2B referral demand is supported by audience and regulatory evidence—not exact volume. No DrawFlow case files were supplied. No installed artifact identified itself as NotFair; the actual method source was the Codex SEO Universal SEO Analysis Skill v1.9.6.

11

One page becomes an evidence system.

A minimal-footprint company wins by making each approved topic unusually complete, maintainable, and distributable. The canonical HTML page is the source of truth; every derivative asset inherits its sources, claims, reviewer, and update date.

Canonical core
Source-backed HTML decision guide

Direct answer, assumptions, exceptions, official links, decision tree, and one next step.

Original utility
Calculator, checklist, spreadsheet, or navigator

Give the searcher a repeatable task outcome—not another opinion article.

Media twin
Video, transcript, diagrams, and clips

Compete in video/images and create clean answer units for humans and AI systems.

Proof layer
Primary sources, cases, reviewers, update log

Every important claim has an owner, source, freshness rule, and explicit limitation.

Distribution
Partners and transparent community participation

Earn review, citations, questions, and referrals. No synthetic Reddit, mass outreach, or link schemes.

Learning loop
Search + engagement + qualified-project outcomes

Measure impressions, utility use, assisted paths, assessment starts, project fit, and funded outcomes.
