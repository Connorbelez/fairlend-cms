## Objective

Validate a repeatable, budget-governed OpenSEO and NotFair research workflow for qualified Toronto/GTA project-financing opportunities without changing the website, Payload CMS, repository source, platform settings, or external accounts.

The trial covers four representative workstreams:

1. five-plus-unit multiplex and MLI Select opportunities;
2. garden and laneway-suite financing;
3. DrawFlow and builder financing; and
4. B2B mortgage-broker, realtor, and builder referral demand.

## Commercial outcome

The primary outcome is a qualified project-financing assessment from a prospect who owns or has identified a GTA property, has credible equity or down-payment capital, intends to acquire/convert/build within 12 months, intends to create income-producing units, needs project financing, and is willing to provide documentation and undergo underwriting.

The positioning under test is end-to-end project partnership from acquisition through construction and long-term takeout, with five-plus-unit build-to-hold projects receiving dominant multiplex weighting.

## Trial boundaries

- The included US$1 balance is the complete budget.
- Paid-data spend must stop at or below US$0.90.
- Toronto is the primary live-search market; GTA is secondary.
- OpenSEO supplies live keyword/SERP evidence and canonical research state.
- NotFair supplies research, clustering, page-planning, GEO, and no-copy briefing methods.
- Page architecture below the homepage is treated as greenfield.
- Lead Capture and Authority Build opportunities are scored and ranked separately.
- The DrawFlow up-to-50% interest-savings claim remains provisional until underlying case studies and calculations are supplied and approved.
- The run stops at content-ready research briefs. It must not produce finished marketing copy, implementation diffs, or CMS-ready content.
- All future site changes require separate proposal documents and explicit human authorization.

## Required outcome

The completed trial must provide provenance-bearing keyword, cluster, SERP, competitor, page-opportunity, AI-question, evidence, claim, and content-brief artifacts; a spend/run manifest; a greenfield page map; an executive strategy; and a full-scale research estimate.
