# Requirements brief — Ontario construction-financing requirements and first-time developer readiness

**Status:** proposal only; not finished copy  
**Priority:** P2  
**Proposed canonical path:** `/resources/construction-financing-requirements-ontario/`

## Search task

Help a borrower understand what a construction-financing file must prove, identify gaps early, and assemble the property, borrower, team, budget, approval, draw, and exit evidence required for an informed review.

## Evidence

- `construction loans canada`: observed volume 140, difficulty 0.
- `construction financing canada`: 140, difficulty 17.
- `construction loan toronto`: 90, difficulty 0.
- Exact requirements/down-payment/equity/liquidity/document phrases returned no row or null volume; they remain valuable questions, not volume claims.

## Required modules

- REQUIREMENT: Explain lender decision areas: borrower/guarantor, equity/liquidity, property/land, experience/team, plans/approvals, budget/contingency, contract, valuation, schedule, draws, risks, exit/takeout.
- REQUIREMENT: State that no universal down-payment/equity/liquidity threshold applies across lenders and scenarios.
- REQUIREMENT: Provide a document/readiness checklist with “available / in progress / gap / owner / next action.”
- REQUIREMENT: Add first-time developer mitigants: experienced team, realistic scope, stronger equity/liquidity, fixed/verified contracts, third-party reports, staged approvals, and credible exit—without promising acceptance.
- REQUIREMENT: Map conventional, alternative/private, insured takeout, and rescue paths at a category level.
- REQUIREMENT: Explain acquisition-plus-construction, stalled project, bank decline, modular deposits, and multiple-project working-capital as separate scenarios.
- REQUIREMENT: Route builders with draw cash-flow pain to the draw toolkit; route 5+ build-to-hold projects to MLI readiness.

## Answer targets

- What documents are required?
- How much equity/liquidity might be required and why does it vary?
- Can a first-time developer qualify?
- Can acquisition and construction be financed together?
- What changes after a bank decline or stalled project?
- How do construction and takeout underwriting differ?

## Required evidence and reviewers

- EVIDENCE: current lender/product documentation and anonymized file patterns;
- REVIEW: underwriter/broker, construction/QS, valuation, legal, compliance;
- REVIEW: no sensitive-document collection until an approved secure intake exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- no universal qualification promise;
- scenarios are separated rather than collapsed into generic advice;
- checklists identify evidence owner and freshness;
- links connect to draw, MLI, multiplex, garden-suite, modular, and rescue resources without creating keyword-cannibalizing clones;
- tracking distinguishes builder, citizen developer, investor, partner, and stalled-project intent.
