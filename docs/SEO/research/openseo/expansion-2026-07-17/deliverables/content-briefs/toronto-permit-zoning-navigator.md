# Requirements brief — Toronto adding-units permit and zoning navigator

**Status:** proposal only; not finished copy  
**Priority:** P1  
**Proposed canonical path:** `/resources/toronto-adding-units-permit-zoning-guide/`

## Search task

Help a non-expert use official Toronto tools to perform an initial property/project screen, identify the professional and municipal path, and understand what evidence a financing team will need next.

## Evidence

- `toronto building permit`: observed volume 2,400, difficulty 2.
- `development applications toronto`: 1,900, difficulty 0.
- `toronto zoning` and `toronto zoning bylaw`: 1,600 each, difficulty 0.
- Official City pages own the authoritative facts and tools. FairLend should add project-financing workflow and interpretation, not duplicate them.

## Required modules

- REQUIREMENT: “Start with your address” workflow linking to zoning, Application Information Centre, permit/status, property information, garden/laneway/multiplex guides, and professional review.
- REQUIREMENT: Explain what each official tool can and cannot tell the user.
- REQUIREMENT: Add a unit-count/project-type decision tree: internal unit, garden suite, laneway suite, 2–4 multiplex, 5+ multi-unit/low-rise apartment.
- REQUIREMENT: Map municipal steps to financing evidence: property control, concept, zoning opinion/review, design, budget, approvals, appraisal, construction, inspections, lease-up, takeout.
- REQUIREMENT: Explain status labels by linking to the City glossary; never predict timing.
- REQUIREMENT: Include comparable-project/application research as a clue, not proof of approval.
- REQUIREMENT: Provide a property-screening checklist and professional-team handoff.

## Answer targets

- How do I check Toronto zoning?
- Where do I find nearby development applications?
- What does a permit status mean?
- Which adding-units guide applies to my project?
- When do I need a planner, designer, engineer, arborist, lawyer, appraiser, builder, and financing team?
- What should be known before waiving a purchase condition?

## Evidence and reviewers

- EVIDENCE: current City of Toronto official pages only for municipal facts;
- REVIEW: Toronto planner/designer, construction/financing, legal where approval implications appear;
- REFRESH: 90 days maximum, and immediately after material by-law/process changes.

## Search boundary

Do not build thin pages for `toronto zoning`, `toronto building permit`, and `development applications toronto` independently. Use one canonical navigator plus tightly justified project-type modules. Do not scrape/rehost official data without authorization.

## Acceptance criteria

- every municipal claim links to the official source;
- no approval/timeline promise;
- no stale screenshot without date/source;
- mobile address-first workflow is usable;
- decision tree clearly separates 2–4 from 5+;
- CTA is “screen this property/project,” not a generic lead form.
