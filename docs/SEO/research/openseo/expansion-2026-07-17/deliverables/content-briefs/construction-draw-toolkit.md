# Requirements brief — Ontario construction draw schedule and DrawFlow toolkit

**Status:** proposal only; not finished copy  
**Priority:** P1  
**Proposed canonical path:** `/resources/construction-loan-draw-schedule-ontario/`

## Search task

Show a builder/developer when money becomes available under a conventional construction loan, where real deposits and completed-work reimbursements create gaps, and what schedule/budget evidence is needed to compare alternatives.

## Evidence

- `construction loan draw schedule`: observed volume 10, difficulty 40.
- Template and Excel variants each returned observed volume 10; difficulty was unavailable/47.
- Live Toronto SERP: AI Overview, Ontario niche publisher above RBC, PAA, PDF/XLS, images, calculator, video/YouTube, Procore, WOWA, lender/broker pages.
- The final broad competitor lookup found YouTube across all seven higher-funnel clusters.

## Required modules

- REQUIREMENT: Define draw schedule, progress draw, inspection, borrower equity-first funding, reimbursement, holdback, contingency, interest reserve, and cost-to-complete in plain language.
- REQUIREMENT: Provide a worked schedule with editable amounts, dates, milestones, deposits, inspections, holdback, interest, and cumulative borrower cash exposure.
- REQUIREMENT: Provide downloadable spreadsheet and printable schedule; visible HTML explanation remains canonical.
- REQUIREMENT: Show deposits for windows, kitchens, HVAC, modular/off-site work, materials, and trades before visible completion.
- REQUIREMENT: Separate lender mechanics from Ontario statutory holdback/lien concepts and require legal review.
- REQUIREMENT: Compare conventional reimbursement draws with DrawFlow at the mechanism level: availability growth, verification, borrower-controlled timing, interest on drawn funds, and qualification limits.
- REQUIREMENT: Insert case-study slots but keep savings metrics hidden until evidence files are supplied and reproduced.
- REQUIREMENT: Link to builder financing assessment and budget-readiness resources.

## Answer targets

- How does a construction loan draw schedule work?
- What milestones normally trigger a draw?
- Why can a project be cash-flow positive on paper but unable to pay deposits?
- How do inspection delays, holdback, contingency, and interest affect available cash?
- What should a draw schedule template contain?
- How does on-demand availability differ from reimbursement?

## Required evidence and reviewers

- EVIDENCE: representative lender draw requirements with permission/attribution;
- EVIDENCE: current Ontario Construction Act interpretation from qualified counsel;
- EVIDENCE: builder/QS-reviewed example budget;
- EVIDENCE: DrawFlow product documentation and eligibility;
- EVIDENCE: supplied case files before any savings claim;
- REVIEW: legal, construction finance, product, compliance, spreadsheet QA.

## Multi-format requirements

- HTML worked example;
- editable XLSX/CSV template;
- cash-exposure calculator;
- draw timeline and cumulative-cash images;
- long-form walkthrough video, short clips, transcript;
- PAA-style visible answer blocks.

## Claims locked

- `up to 50%` or any savings percentage until case evidence passes;
- universal number/timing of draws;
- promise of on-demand access, approval, rate, interest savings, or project completion;
- legal interpretation without counsel review.

## Acceptance criteria

- spreadsheet formulas and edge cases tested;
- example reconciles to 100% of sources/uses;
- drawn balance and interest logic visible;
- conventional/DrawFlow comparison is fair and qualification-dependent;
- source/version metadata embedded;
- assessment CTA captures project type, budget, current stage, and cash-flow issue without soliciting unapproved sensitive data.
