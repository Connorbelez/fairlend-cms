## Directory map

| Directory | Purpose | Organization rule |
|---|---|---|
| [`master-report/`](master-report/) | The consolidated, source-audited report and its delivery metadata. | One dated master package per major research cycle. |
| [`research/keyword-and-funnel/`](research/keyword-and-funnel/) | Keyword universes, five-layer funnel assignments, page clusters, questions, SERP evidence, GSC/GA4 validation, and content outlines. | Preserve dated research packages intact. |
| [`research/competitive/`](research/competitive/) | Market landscape, competitor positioning, content patterns, and strategic differentiation. | Store source reports; visual derivatives live under `visual-reports/`. |
| [`research/openseo/`](research/openseo/) | Governed OpenSEO trials and expansions, including raw inputs, argument payloads, contracts, workspaces, and deliverables. | Keep each experiment's workspace and deliverables together beneath its dated directory. |
| [`strategy/`](strategy/) | SEO audits, operating playbooks, launch implementation, and tooling strategy. | Durable operating documents rather than dated datasets. |
| [`measurement-and-indexing/`](measurement-and-indexing/) | Analytics setup, vendor status, Search Console/indexing launch procedures, and indexing-engine notes. | Operational measurement and discovery infrastructure. |
| [`authority-and-distribution/`](authority-and-distribution/) | Community demand, journalist/media opportunities, digital PR evidence, calculators, and editorial data assets. | Separate outreach research from on-site content assets. |
| [`references/`](references/) | Primary-source notes, platform research, AI-answer research, Reddit research, and official-source packs. | Evidence and implementation references, not final strategies. |
| [`visual-reports/`](visual-reports/) | Self-contained HTML reports, QA images, and visual-report build support. | Visual derivatives only; canonical source data remains in its research package. |

## Maintenance rules

1. Put new dated datasets and research runs under the relevant `research/` category.
2. Keep raw evidence, normalized data, QA, manifests, and reports together inside the dated package.
3. Put durable process documents under `strategy/` or `measurement-and-indexing/`, not inside dated research packages.
4. Put visual derivatives under `visual-reports/` and link back to their canonical source package.
5. Run `node scripts/research/build-seo-docs-index.mjs` after adding or relocating files.
6. Do not delete `.agent/diagrams` copies used as published prototype sources; repository copies are the durable documentation versions.

See [Migration map](MIGRATION-MAP.md) for the old-to-new locations used in the July 20 consolidation.
