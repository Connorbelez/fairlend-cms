# FairLend OpenSEO expansion — secondary and higher-funnel research

**Research date:** 2026-07-17  
**Market:** Canada metrics; Toronto live SERPs; GTA business scope  
**Status:** proposal/research only — no content, CMS, site, search-platform, analytics, or deployment mutation authorized

This package is the second research wave after the USD 1 OpenSEO trial. It uses the trial's remaining governed headroom to find answerable, utility-led search opportunities that a new domain can pursue before it has the authority to win broad commercial mortgage queries.

## Result

- Six new OpenSEO discovery seeds returned 756 rows; 45 relevant rows survived geography/category filtering.
- A 102-query exact portfolio produced 29 provider rows; all 29 returned `searchVolume: null`, so absence/null remains unavailable—not zero.
- The normalized universe contains 130 relevant discovered and proposed long-tails across nine clusters.
- Three Toronto live SERPs persisted successfully: garden-suite cost, MLI Select requirements, and construction-loan draw schedule.
- Those SERPs contain 60 elements: 45 organic results, three AI Overviews, three People Also Ask blocks, three related-search blocks, plus video, Reddit/forums, images, PDFs, calculators, and primary-government pages.
- YouTube was the only domain overlapping all seven broad informational clusters in the final competitor lookup.
- Expansion spend was USD 0.44716 of a USD 0.44756 ceiling. USD 0.00040 of ceiling headroom and the protected USD 0.12 provider reserve remain.

## Content boundary

Every proposed page, tool, video, download, community post, schema type, CTA, and internal link is a requirement for a later content-production proposal. Nothing in this package is finished marketing copy or permission to create/update Payload records. Program, planning, cost, incentive, rate, eligibility, and performance claims require the evidence/review gates stated in the briefs.

## Relationship to the first trial

The original package at `fairlend-openseo-trial-2026-07-16` remains the canonical commercial-path and greenfield-architecture research. This expansion does not replace it. It adds the research/awareness layer that was intentionally cut from the first USD 1 trial: cost, feasibility, permits, zoning, requirements, documentation, draw mechanics, templates, calculators, video, Reddit/forum surfaces, and GTA source navigation.
