# Executive findings

## Decision

FairLend should not try to bootstrap authority by publishing generic mortgage articles. The attainable wedge is **decision-support utility at the exact moment a homeowner, builder, investor, or referral partner discovers that the project is more complicated than the property idea**.

The next organic layer should answer:

1. Can this property support the intended units?
2. What approvals, documents, capital, professionals, and timing are required?
3. What will the project cost and where do cash-flow gaps appear?
4. How do early design and budget decisions affect 5+ unit MLI/takeout eligibility?
5. When is conventional draw financing a poor match for real project cash flow?

That is FairLend's one-stop-shop promise translated into search demand: not “we offer mortgages,” but “we help determine, structure, fund, build, stabilize, and take out the project.”

## What changed from the first trial

The first trial correctly found four commercial paths: 5+ unit/MLI, builders/DrawFlow, garden/laneway suites, and professional partners. It intentionally underfunded the research layer. This expansion validates the missing acquisition surface.

### 1. Municipal navigation is the largest measured awareness seam

Canada-level discovery metrics returned:

- `toronto building permit` — 2,400 volume, difficulty 2;
- `development applications toronto` — 1,900, difficulty 0;
- `toronto zoning` — 1,600, difficulty 0;
- `toronto zoning bylaw` — 1,600, difficulty 0.

These are **not** invitations to outrank the City of Toronto with a generic permit page. The opportunity is the intersection the City does not own: how to use official tools to screen a rental-development property, what a financing file needs before/after each approval, what changes at 5+ units, and when to bring in FairLend's partner network.

### 2. Garden-suite cost is a multi-surface SERP, not an article contest

The live Toronto SERP for `garden suite cost toronto` contained:

- AI Overview at the first element;
- Reddit as the first organic result;
- People Also Ask;
- a discussion/forum block;
- video;
- cost calculators;
- local builders and specialty publishers;
- a knowledge graph and reviews.

The ranking pages quote materially different cost ranges. A defensible asset therefore needs transparent assumptions, a dated range model, soft/hard-cost separation, servicing/site-condition variables, a municipal source layer, rent/financing scenarios, and clear “estimate—not quote” language. A prose-only page will be structurally incomplete.

### 3. MLI Select requires primary-source interpretation

The live `cmhc mli select requirements` SERP leads with CMHC's program page and PDF. AI Overview, PAA, video, engineering, lender, broker, GTA, Reddit, documentation, and application guides follow.

The opportunity is not to rewrite CMHC. It is to connect official requirements to choices made before construction: unit count, affordability period, energy target, accessibility/visitability, net worth/liquidity, documentation, bonding/draw process, operating assumptions, stabilization, and takeout timing. This is the strategic bridge to FairLend's heavily preferred 5+ unit files.

### 4. Draw schedules are a winnable builder-utility lane

The live `construction loan draw schedule` SERP contained AI Overview, PAA, images, PDFs/spreadsheets, video, calculators, niche Ontario publishers, RBC, Procore, WOWA, and lender/broker explainers. A niche Ontario site ranked above RBC.

FairLend's strongest differentiated asset is a worked schedule and downloadable template that shows:

- conventional reimbursement timing;
- deposits/material/trade cash needed before inspection;
- holdback/contingency/interest-reserve treatment;
- cumulative borrower cash exposure;
- a clearly labelled DrawFlow comparison;
- case-study proof once supplied.

The `up to 50% felt-interest savings` claim remains locked until the underlying case files are supplied and reviewed. The tool can be designed now; the claim cannot be published now.

### 5. Video is a distribution requirement

The final broad-cluster competitor call found YouTube across all seven inputs: zoning, permits, development applications, garden suites, MLI Select, construction loans, and construction financing. Each validated SERP also exposed video or YouTube. Every flagship asset should therefore produce a short explainer, a longer walkthrough, diagrams/stills, transcript, and embeddable clips.

## Minimum-footprint launch sequence

1. Publish one source-backed flagship utility, not five thin posts.
2. Add the matching checklist/template/calculator and an HTML explanation on the same canonical topic.
3. Record the matching video and embed it; publish the transcript and diagrams.
4. Use Reddit/forums for question discovery and transparent expert participation—not link dropping or synthetic posts.
5. Ask builders, planners, designers, engineers, realtors, and municipal/resource partners to review or contribute specific evidence units.
6. Internally link the research asset to exactly one next-step service path and one proof asset.
7. Measure impressions, non-brand queries, engaged tool use, qualified assessment starts, and assisted leads before expanding the cluster.

## What not to publish

- generic “what is a mortgage” or “bad credit mortgage” content;
- cloned municipality pages with swapped city names;
- unverified cost, rent, ROI, savings, rate, leverage, approval-time, or qualification promises;
- an MLI page that paraphrases CMHC without explaining project decisions;
- a DrawFlow comparison before evidence for the savings claim is available;
- AI-generated Reddit posts, fake user stories, or community seeding;
- dozens of zero/unavailable-volume long-tails as separate pages.

## Definition of a successful first cluster

The first cluster is working when one canonical asset earns impressions across multiple related questions, appears in at least one non-standard SERP surface (PAA/video/images/discussions/AI citation), drives repeated use of its utility/download, produces qualified project-assessment starts, and can be updated from a maintained evidence register without rewriting the whole page.
