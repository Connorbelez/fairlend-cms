# Requirements brief — CMHC MLI Select requirements and 5+ unit readiness

**Status:** proposal only; not finished copy  
**Priority:** P1  
**Proposed canonical path:** `/resources/cmhc-mli-select-requirements/`

## Search task

Translate current CMHC requirements into the decisions a GTA borrower and professional team must make before acquisition/design/construction so the eventual 5+ unit takeout strategy is not treated as an afterthought.

## Evidence

- `cmhc mli select requirements`: observed volume 30.
- `mli select requirements`: 20.
- Live Toronto SERP: official CMHC page and PDF at positions 1–2; AI Overview, PAA, video, engineering, lender, broker, GTA, Reddit, documentation, and application guides follow.
- The official program currently states a five-unit minimum for ordinary projects and uses affordability, energy, and accessibility points; all details are time-sensitive.

## Boundary

- REQUIREMENT: Keep 2–4-unit residential multiplex guidance separate from the 5+ unit MLI/takeout path.
- REQUIREMENT: Link to CMHC primary sources; do not present FairLend as CMHC or an approval authority.
- REQUIREMENT: Explain construction financing and insured takeout as coordinated but distinct underwriting stages.

## Required modules

- REQUIREMENT: Direct answer and “who this is for/not for.”
- REQUIREMENT: Five-unit threshold and project-type boundary.
- REQUIREMENT: Points matrix covering affordability, energy, accessibility/visitability, commitment term, and current flexibility tiers.
- REQUIREMENT: Design-stage decision map showing which choices become expensive or impossible to fix later.
- REQUIREMENT: Net-worth, liquidity, borrower experience, guarantees, documentation, bonding/draw, appraisal, environmental, budget, and operating evidence—clearly labelled as program/lender/case dependent.
- REQUIREMENT: Construction-to-stabilization-to-takeout timeline.
- REQUIREMENT: Readiness checklist with owner, professional, lender, and timing columns.
- REQUIREMENT: Common failure modes and remediation paths.
- REQUIREMENT: update log with CMHC source revision date.

## Answer targets

- What are the current MLI Select requirements?
- How many units are required?
- How do points affect leverage/amortization/premium flexibility?
- What affordability, energy, and accessibility choices must be planned early?
- What net worth, liquidity, and documents may be required?
- Can MLI Select fund construction, or is it the takeout strategy?
- When should the approved lender/CMHC path be tested?

## Required evidence and reviewers

- EVIDENCE: current CMHC MLI Select HTML page and fact sheet;
- EVIDENCE: current Required Documentation Guide and forms;
- EVIDENCE: current approved-lender interpretation for construction/takeout workflow;
- REVIEW: CMHC-experienced lender/broker, energy consultant, accessibility/design professional, valuation/underwriting, compliance;
- REFRESH: 30 days maximum for program numbers and requirements.

## Multi-format requirements

- requirements matrix;
- 2–4 versus 5+ decision tree;
- design-to-takeout lifecycle diagram;
- downloadable readiness checklist;
- points explainer/calculator only if arithmetic and source version are testable;
- video and transcript.

## Claims locked

- guaranteed eligibility, leverage, amortization, rate, approval, timing, or premium;
- using stale point thresholds or code/standard references;
- presenting an illustrative scenario as a commitment;
- implying FairLend alone controls the insured takeout decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- every program number maps to a current CMHC source;
- 2–4/5+ boundary is explicit;
- construction-versus-takeout distinction is explicit;
- checklist has source/reviewer/date metadata;
- compliance and subject-matter review complete;
- tracking captures checklist use and qualified 5+ project assessments.
