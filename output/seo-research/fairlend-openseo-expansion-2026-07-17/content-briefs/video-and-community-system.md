# Requirements brief — video, Reddit/forum, and AI-answer distribution system

**Status:** proposal only; not a publication calendar or authorization  
**Priority:** P1 supporting system

## Evidence

- YouTube was the only competitor domain overlapping all seven broad higher-funnel inputs.
- Garden cost SERP exposed video, Reddit as first organic, and a forum block.
- MLI requirements exposed video and Reddit.
- Draw schedules exposed images and a YouTube organic result.
- Every successful representative SERP contained AI Overview and People Also Ask.

## Canonical-first rule

Each topic has one reviewed canonical HTML asset. Video, transcript, clips, diagrams, spreadsheets, partner handouts, and community answers are derivative evidence units. They cannot introduce new claims or numbers that are absent from the canonical source register.

## Video package requirement

- REQUIREMENT: one 8–15 minute walkthrough per flagship asset;
- REQUIREMENT: chaptered transcript in HTML;
- REQUIREMENT: three short clips answering distinct questions;
- REQUIREMENT: thumbnail and diagrams derived from the actual model/checklist;
- REQUIREMENT: visible reviewer/source/update metadata;
- REQUIREMENT: proposed `VideoObject` only when the video is public, embedded, and metadata matches.

## Reddit/forum operating rule

- REQUIREMENT: monitor questions and vocabulary; do not automate posting.
- REQUIREMENT: participate only through an authorized, identifiable human account.
- REQUIREMENT: disclose affiliation when relevant.
- REQUIREMENT: answer the question completely; link only when the resource materially adds utility and the community rules permit it.
- PROHIBITED: synthetic personas, vote manipulation, mass posting, hidden promotion, copied answers, fabricated case stories, or “parasite SEO” pages.

## AI-answer requirements

- direct answer first, then assumptions and exceptions;
- original tables/calculators/checklists with visible methodology;
- primary sources adjacent to claims;
- explicit entity/role/licence details and reviewer attribution;
- stable anchors, descriptive headings, concise definitions, and update history;
- visible HTML must match any proposed structured data;
- no keyword/question stuffing.

## Measurement

- video impressions, qualified views, and assisted page visits;
- non-brand impressions across canonical and transcript queries;
- image/video/PAA/AI citation observations;
- community referral quality and branded-search lift;
- calculator/checklist engagement and qualified assessment starts;
- citations/referring domains from professional and local sources.

## Acceptance criteria

- canonical asset approved before derivatives;
- claim parity test passes across page/video/transcript/social/community excerpts;
- captions/transcript/alt text accessible;
- community rules and affiliation disclosure documented;
- no external publication without separate authorization.
