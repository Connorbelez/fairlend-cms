# FairLend footer concept prompt set

Generation mode: built-in `imagegen` (`ui-mockup`), one independent call per concept.

## Shared direction

- Asset: one standalone, high-fidelity desktop website footer in a wide landscape frame. Never a collage, mood board, comparison sheet, browser frame, or set of variants.
- References: the three supplied footer screenshots were used for composition only: a panoramic visual field, large atmospheric typography or scenery, and a disciplined navigation deck.
- Brand source: `DESIGN.md` — “The Toronto Financing Field Guide.”
- Material: warm paper (`#F8F7F5`, `#FFFDF9`), deep ink (`#030405`, `#08090A`), muted ink (`#494944`, `#6C6C64`), paper rules (`#DEDED8`), and sparse signal lime (`#96EC18`).
- Image treatment: Toronto, construction, property, maps, parcels, infrastructure, or underwriting evidence rendered in black/charcoal engraving, stipple, crosshatch, or screened halftone. Texture stays inside image regions; reading surfaces remain clean.
- Typography: Cormorant-style serif for authority, Inter-style sans for navigation and operation, Oxanium-style labels only for real technical metadata.
- Layout: cinematic image-led upper field; squared capital-desk grid below; serif closing proposition; practical navigation; regulatory/legal rail; square lime conversion action.
- Lime rule: under 10% of visual mass and used only for route, action, active state, verification, or direction.
- Avoid: navy-and-gold banking, generic fintech blue, full-colour stock photography, gradients, glassmorphism, rounded SaaS cards, decorative glow, meaningless lime, global grain over copy, watermarks, or invented brand marks.

## 01 — Toronto Capital Horizon

Create a panoramic monochrome engraved Toronto waterfront with the CN Tower, mid-rise construction cranes, and halftone clouds. Let the panorama dissolve into a squared capital-desk grid. Put the FairLend wordmark and “Capital built for the way Toronto builds.” in the large left cell; Build, Borrow, Invest, and Company navigation in the centre; and “Start a conversation” with a paper email field and square lime arrow at right. Add a thin legal baseline with Toronto, FSRA, Privacy, and Terms. Use one lime route line and verified node.

## 02 — The Underwriting Atlas

Create a monumental monochrome topographic map of the Greater Toronto Area with parcel lines, shoreline, contour marks, and a faint Toronto skyline. Run one dashed lime capital route through three verified nodes. Transition through a slim proposition rail reading “Find the route. Fund the build.” into an asymmetric squared grid with Financing, Investing, Resources, Company, and a “Talk to a capital expert” conversion cell. Use technical coordinate and licence metadata as genuine map labels.

## 03 — The Monumental Wordmark

Set enormous cropped serif letters spelling “FAIRLEND” across the upper field in light charcoal. Partially occlude them with layered construction drawings and folded site plans that form a sculptural foreground containing parcel contours, cranes, and Toronto rooflines. Carry one lime financing route across the folds to a verified endpoint. Below, use a restrained link deck with the proposition “FINANCING THAT MOVES WITH THE BUILD.” and a square lime “Start your file” action.

## 04 — The Closing Dossier

Build the footer around one large open underwriting dossier viewed straight-on. Fill it with a monochrome halftone construction photograph, Toronto parcel diagram, deal stamps, financing figures, and a CN Tower crop. Trace a sparse lime progress line across “01 PLAN”, “02 FINANCE”, “03 BUILD”, “04 DRAWS”, “05 TAKEOUT”, and “06 OUTCOME”, ending in a verified mark. Below, organize navigation as file-tab grid cells around “Every project deserves a clear path to capital.” and a square lime “Open a file” action.

NEITHER DOES THE BUILD.” in the squared black navigation deck, with accessible bone-paper type, a white-paper email input, and square lime submit action.

## 08 — The Toronto Closing Page

Create the quietest, most spacious direction. Use a minimal engraved Lake Ontario horizon with Toronto Islands, CN Tower, neighbourhoods, cranes, and stippled clouds. Below, use only three navigation columns and a strong “Talk to FairLend” phone action, followed by Toronto, FSRA, copyright, Privacy, and Terms.
