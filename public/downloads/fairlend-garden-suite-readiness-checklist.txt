FAIRLEND GARDEN SUITE PROJECT READINESS CHECKLIST
=================================================

You do not need everything before talking to FairLend. This checklist shows what a complete project file eventually contains.

[ ] PROPERTY
    Title, existing mortgage statements and property details.

[ ] BORROWER
    Income, credit, liquidity and obligations.

[ ] PLANS AND PERMITS
    Drawings, permit status and professional reports.

[ ] BUDGET
    Line items, contingency, contracts, quotes and deposits.

[ ] TEAM
    Builder, contractor, trade and supplier commitments.

[ ] DRAWFLOW
    Schedule and milestone evidence.

[ ] INSURANCE AND SITE
    Coverage and site documentation.

[ ] APPRAISAL AND RENT
    Value and rental evidence.

[ ] COMPLETION
    Takeout plan and end-state financing.

Starting this checklist is not a financing approval or commitment. Requirements depend on the property, borrower, project, lender and current program rules.
