## Section 2 — What a Private Mortgage Investment Is

**Section goal:** Educate without patronizing. Explain in plain language what a private mortgage is, how an investor earns income, and why the investment requires underwriting — not just collateral.

### Concept A — Plain-language definition card
**Layout/aesthetic:** A single, calm definition block in a wide measure, with three supporting "how it works" tiles beneath (Secured by property · You earn the interest · Shorter terms). Restrained, textbook-clear, generous white space. Reads like a glossary entry from a serious institution.

**Marketing copy:**
- Headline: **What is a private mortgage investment?**
- Body: A private mortgage is a loan secured against real estate. The borrower receives financing outside the traditional bank channel, and investors can earn income from the interest paid on that mortgage. The security, valuation, borrower quality, LTV, payment structure, exit path, and administration process determine whether the opportunity is worth considering.
- Tile 1 — *Secured by property:* Your investment is backed by registered mortgage security against real estate — not an unsecured promise to pay.
- Tile 2 — *You earn the interest:* Income comes from the interest the borrower pays over the mortgage term.
- Tile 3 — *Shorter by design:* Private mortgage terms are typically shorter than conventional mortgages.

### Concept B — "Where the yield comes from" explainer
**Layout/aesthetic:** Two-column editorial. Left: the definition. Right: a short, honest list answering "why does this pay more?" Each item pairs a reason with the risk it implies, set as a small ledger. The candor is the design — yield and risk shown on the same line.

**Marketing copy:**
- Headline: **Higher income, and the reason behind it.**
- Body: Private mortgages can pay higher interest because they solve real financing needs banks often can't handle efficiently — speed, bridge timing, construction stages, complex income, or non-standard documentation.
- Ledger rows (reason → what it means for you):
  - Speed and flexibility → shorter terms, less liquidity
  - Complex borrower or property → more underwriting required
  - Non-bank situations → risk must be priced, documented, and managed
- Closing line: Higher yield does not mean no risk. It means the risk must be priced, documented, monitored, and managed.

### Concept C — Anatomy of a private mortgage diagram
**Layout/aesthetic:** A clean line-art diagram showing the relationship: Borrower → Property (security) → Mortgage → Investor (income), with FairLend as the administration layer in the middle. Labels are short; the diagram does the teaching. High contrast, one accent on the investor node.

**Marketing copy:**
- Headline: **How a private mortgage actually works.**
- Diagram labels: Borrower needs financing · Property secures the loan · Investor provides capital, earns interest · FairLend underwrites, documents, administers, and monitors
- Supporting line: The collateral matters — but so does everything around it. A mortgage is only as conservative as the valuation, the LTV, the borrower, and the administration behind it.
- Footnote: A private mortgage is not a bank deposit. It is mortgage-backed private credit, with real security and real risk.
