# FairLend Partnership Operations

This context covers how FairLend researches, qualifies, contacts, and develops prospective distribution and referral partners.

## Language

**Partner Lead**:
An organization or professional being evaluated for a FairLend referral, distribution, media, community, or event partnership.
_Avoid_: Account, prospect record

**Follow-up Action**:
An owned, scheduled activity that advances a Partner Lead and records its due time, contact method, completion state, and execution requirements.
_Avoid_: Reminder, next step, scheduled follow-up

**Next Follow-up Snapshot**:
The Partner Lead's denormalized projection of its earliest open Follow-up Action, used for queueing, filtering, and prioritization.
_Avoid_: Follow-up Action, reminder

**Follow-up Requirements**:
The lead-specific information, evidence, asset, or response that must be sent, requested, verified, or captured before a Follow-up Action can be completed.
_Avoid_: Notes, generic instructions

**Public Contact Route**:
An attributable public method for reaching a Partner Lead or its decision-maker, such as a business email, telephone number, contact form, or professional profile.
_Avoid_: Contact details, private contact

**Build Portfolio Evidence**:
Attributable public evidence of a builder's completed, active, or announced projects and the services associated with them.
_Avoid_: Portfolio claim, build list

**MLI Select Early-file Professional**:
A professional whose ordinary project work exposes multifamily feasibility, site, design, energy, accessibility, cost, planning, valuation, or development evidence before the financing structure is finalized.
_Avoid_: MLI contact, industry-adjacent professional

**MLI Select Touchpoint**:
The specific project decision, document, analysis, or approval through which an MLI Select Early-file Professional encounters a prospective qualifying file.
_Avoid_: Connection, involvement
